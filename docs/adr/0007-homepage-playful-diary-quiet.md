# ADR 0007: The homepage plays, the diary page stays quiet

## Decision

Funny and experimental touches go on the homepage. The diary page (`/guests/[id]`) stays toned down: the guest, the stay and the entries, with nothing that reacts to the outside world.

## Context

The homepage and the diary page started to look alike, both a hero over a list of entries. They do different jobs. The homepage is about today: which dog is here and what has happened so far. The diary page is the record of a whole stay, read back later by the guest's human and by Alan and Weiwen.

Today-only things only make sense on the homepage, and that's where the playful stuff has landed:

- Pat a dog in the hero and it hops and says something.
- When the PSI is unhealthy, grey smog drifts over the hero (#58).
- The row above today's entries reads "Hazy Saturday" or "Sunny Sunday", with the count of today's moments on the right (#57, #59).

## Consequences

New playful ideas (weather, sounds, jokes, live data, odd animations) are pitched for the homepage. If one is aimed at the diary page, question it first. Diary page changes favour calm and readability over novelty.
